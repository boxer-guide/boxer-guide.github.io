Privacy Policy

Boxer Guide is published as static pages through GitHub Pages. No registration, no newsletter and no contact forms means no personal data reaches us.

Cookies: none. Your browser may store a reading counter or calculator inputs in localStorage, and that data stays on your device. GitHub may keep technical logs such as IP addresses according to its privacy statement.

Video embeds use youtube-nocookie.com. External links lead to sites with separate policies. Reach us via contact.

      How the line-up is picked

      The generator holds 148 names, each tagged boy, girl or either and sorted into styles. Your settings filter the list, and eight matching names are drawn at random. Shuffle again for another eight. If fewer than eight match, you see all of them and a hint to widen the search.

      “Coat colour” adds names that suit the coat you picked, and names for white markings when that box is ticked. The AKC breed page traces the modern Boxer to Germany in the late 1800s, hence the German classics, and says Boxers “move like the athletes they are named for”, hence the champion names.

      Choosing between finalists

      • Say it out loud. Call each finalist the way you would across a park. The one that feels natural to shout usually wins.
      • Listen for your cues. A name that rhymes with a cue you use, such as Kit and Sit, can be confusing in training.
      • Ask the family. Share the card or the link and let everyone vote.
      • Try it for a day. Use the favourite for a day before you put it on a tag.

      What happens to the card

      The card is drawn in your browser. Nothing you type is sent to us, and there is no account. The shortlist is kept in this browser only. The share link carries the card settings in the part of the address after the #, so anyone who opens it sees the same card and can change it.

      Once the name is settled, the guides on puppy energy and winter coat fit are a good next read.
